🌳 Realmwalker 🌳

Journey through the Nine Realms of Norse mythology. Every decision matters, and each realm presents a difficult choice. Your courage, wisdom, and a bit of luck will determine whether you become a legendary hero or fall before reaching your destination.

How to Play

• Read each encounter carefully.
• Choose one of the two available actions.
• Each choice has its own Chance of Success.
• Roll the die to determine your fate.
• Keep your health above 0 through all 9 realms to complete your journey.
